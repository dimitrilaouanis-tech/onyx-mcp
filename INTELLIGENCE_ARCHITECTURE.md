## The three elevation techniques the FREE ENGINE uses (onyx_elevate.py), all $0:
1. **Self-consistency** — K diverse samples of the same question (different reasoning lenses)
2. **Critique/debate** — one pass finds cross-draft agreements + conflicts
3. **Best-of-N synthesis** — merges the strongest reasoning into one answer
→ Proven: single free answer punted on a merchant-risk question; elevated gave a full
   structured risk table. Near-frontier quality from cheap calls.
